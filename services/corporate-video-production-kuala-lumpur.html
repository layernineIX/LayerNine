Corporate Video Production in Kuala Lumpur

Full-crew shoots and end-to-end post, delivered by the studio that plans the shoot.

Layer Nine produces corporate and brand video in Kuala Lumpur — real crews, real locations, and a full post-production pipeline, finished by the same studio that plans the shoot. No hand-offs between a production company and a separate edit house.

For brands that need a corporate film, internal comms video, or campaign film shot on a real set.

What's included
  • On-location shoots across Kuala Lumpur and beyond
  • Full-crew photography & videography
  • Colour grading & cinematic finishing
  • Edit, sound, and delivery cut for every channel
