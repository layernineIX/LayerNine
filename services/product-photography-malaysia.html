Product Photography in Malaysia

A full catalogue shot to one consistent standard, styled for e-commerce, social, and campaign use.

Layer Nine shoots product photography for brands across Malaysia — styled sets, consistent lighting, and a repeatable look across an entire catalogue, so every product image feels like it belongs to the same campaign.

For brands that need a full product catalogue shot to one consistent standard.

What's included
  • Studio & on-location product shoots
  • Consistent campaign-ready styling across a full catalogue

  • Optional AI-built backgrounds and scene extensions where useful
