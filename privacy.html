Who we are

Layer Nine Studio ("Layer Nine", "we", "us") is a production studio based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. This policy covers the website at layernine.studio and explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Anything you send us directly — for example your name, email address, and project details, when you use the contact form or email us.
  • Basic technical information collected automatically by our hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages), such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used for security and performance.

This site does not use advertising trackers or sell visitor data. If we add analytics or a form backend in future, this page will be updated first.

How the contact form works

The contact form on this site drafts an email in your own mail application addressed to us — the message is not stored on our servers or by any third party. If we later connect a hosted form service, that service's own privacy terms would apply and this page would say so.

How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a potential project.
  • To keep basic records of client communication.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

This site does not set marketing or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary technical cookies to serve the site securely.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing layernineix@gmail.com.

Contact
