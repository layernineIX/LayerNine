Who we are

Layer Nine Studio ("Layer Nine", "we", "us") is a production studio based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. This policy covers the website at layernine.studio and explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Anything you send us directly — for example your name, email address, and project details, when you use the contact page form, the homepage "get in touch" chat, or email us.
  • Basic technical information collected automatically by our hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages), such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used for security and performance.

This site does not use advertising trackers or sell visitor data. If we add analytics in future, this page will be updated first.

How each contact method works — these are different from each other

This site has two separate ways to get in touch, and they don't work the same way:

  • The form on the Contact page submits your name, email, and message to Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards it to our inbox by email. Web3Forms processes that submission on our behalf and has its own privacy policy, available at web3forms.com/privacy. If that submission fails for any reason, this page opens a drafted email in your own mail app instead, addressed to us.
  • The "get in touch" chat on the homepage works differently: it never submits anything to a server. Selecting chips or typing a message and pressing Send simply drafts an email in your own mail application, addressed to us — nothing is transmitted, stored, or seen by us or any third party until you choose to actually send that email yourself.

How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a potential project.
  • To keep basic records of client communication.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

This site does not set marketing or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary technical cookies to serve the site securely.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing layernineix@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to layernineix@gmail.com.
