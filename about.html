About Layer Nine

A production studio in Kuala Lumpur, founded by a filmmaker who builds real sets and digital scenes side by side.

Layer Nine is a production studio based in Kuala Lumpur.

It's led by Ali, a photographer, filmmaker, and creative director. Every project starts the way a real shoot would — with direction, references, and a point of view — then runs through whichever mix of real filming and digital tools gets it done well.

We're not a prompt shop. Real crews, real cameras, and real locations sit next to AI-built scenes and effects in the same edit — used where they make the work better or faster, never as a substitute for direction.

Layer Nine works across brand film, product content, and ongoing social work for clients — alongside originated brand work like ERATO and SKAI that shows the range of what the studio can build.
